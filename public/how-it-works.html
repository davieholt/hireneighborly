How it works

You bring the people.
We bring everything else.

From the first conversation to a live page and a magnet on every refrigerator, here is exactly what happens and who does what.

Five steps

  1. We talk

    You apply, we read it, and if it looks like a fit we get on a call with you. We are looking for one thing above all: whether you can name businesses owned by your neighbors off the top of your head. That cannot be faked and it tells us everything.

  2. We build your page

    Your neighborhood gets its own web address and its own page, built by us, usually inside a week. You do not touch code, hosting, forms or a shopping cart. Ever.

  3. You put the word out

    Post it in the group. Mention it at the pool. Businesses owned by neighbors fill out the join form themselves. You are not doing data entry, you are making introductions.

  4. We confirm they live there

    Every listing gets checked against the neighborhood, and re-confirmed once a year automatically. That check is the only promise this list makes, so we run it, not you.

  5. The calendar goes out

    Once a year we design, print and mail a school calendar magnet to every household in your neighborhood. Four businesses sponsor it. Your page is on it. It stays on the refrigerator for the whole school year.

The magnet is the part people underestimate

It is a school district calendar. First day, last day, breaks, early outs. A family keeps it because they need the dates, not because they want an ad.

Why it stays up

It is useful

Nobody keeps an ad on the fridge. Everybody keeps the school calendar.

Why it renews

It expires

A school year ends and it is worthless. Sponsors decide every year, on a date.

Why it is scarce

Four spots, forever

More sponsors means a bigger magnet means a drawer instead of a fridge. Four is the number that works.

Why it travels

Every place has a district

This is the part that works in any neighborhood, anywhere.
